# Research notes for the McKinley Road Dental homepage

## Official practice website
Source: [McKinley Road Dental](https://www.mckinleyroaddental.com/)

The official site identifies McKinley Road Dental and Dr. Shawn Sydlowski, and says the practice provides general dentistry to all ages. Dr. Sydlowski describes a patient-centered experience and building strong, lasting relationships with patients as priorities. The site does not publish other staff names or individual biographies, so the Team section names only Dr. Sydlowski and refers to the unnamed office team collectively.

The Ottawa schedule on the official site is Monday 10:30 a.m.–7:30 p.m., Tuesday and Thursday 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., and Friday 8:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. A separate line lists Wenona on Wednesday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.; those hours are not represented as Ottawa hours. The official site lists phone/text at (815) 433-3996 and mckinleyroaddental@yahoo.com.

## Ottawa office details
Source: [Shawn E. Sydlowski, DDS on Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/shawn-e-sydlowski-dds-ottawa#reviews)

Yelp lists the Ottawa office at 111 E McKinley Rd, Ottawa, IL 61350, phone (815) 433-3996, and one 5-star review. The reviewer Kristen B. posted the following on March 5, 2026: “Dr. Shawn is a great Dentist and really cares about his Patients! His staff is friendly and knowledgeable. His office runs on time with very minimal wait times. I am so glad I became a Patient of this Office! Highly recommend!” The page quotes this review once and links to the Yelp listing. It does not claim this single review satisfies a request for three independently verified patient reviews.

## Avoid conflating the Wenona office
The Google Maps destination linked from the official site resolves to Wenona Dental Center at 136 N Chestnut St, Wenona, IL 61377, phone (815) 853-4013. It is a separate practice location and its listing, schedule, and reviews must not be attributed to the Ottawa McKinley Road office.
