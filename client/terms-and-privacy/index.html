Part I · HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices

1. Commitment to Health Information Privacy

At McKinley Road Dental, led by Dr. Shawn Sydlowski, DDS, we treat your privacy and confidential health information with the utmost respect. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of all medical and dental information entrusted to our care.

This Notice of Privacy Practices describes how Protected Health Information (PHI) about you may be used and disclosed by our clinical and administrative staff in our Ottawa, Illinois dental practice, as well as your statutory rights to access and control this information pursuant to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA), the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (HITECH) Act, and the Illinois Medical and Dental Records Statutes (735 ILCS 5/8-2001 et seq.).

Practice Duty Under Federal and State Law

We are required by law to maintain the privacy of your protected health information, to provide you with this notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, to abide by the terms of this notice currently in effect, and to notify affected individuals following a breach of unsecured protected health information.

Health Records

2. Scope of Protected Dental Health Information (PHI)

In the course of providing dental treatment and oral healthcare, McKinley Road Dental creates, maintains, and receives oral health records that specifically identify you. In our dental practice environment, Protected Health Information includes, but is not limited to:

  • Medical & Dental Histories: Information regarding systemic conditions, cardiovascular health, diabetes, bleeding disorders, medications, known allergies, pregnancy, and prior surgical or dental history.
  • Clinical Examination Findings: Intraoral and extraoral exam observations, tooth charting, restorations present, caries risk assessments, occlusion records, and soft-tissue or oral cancer screening documentation.
  • Periodontal Records: Pocket depth probings, clinical attachment loss measurements, bleeding on probing, furcation involvement, and mobility assessments.
  • Diagnostic Radiographs & Images: Digital bitewings, periapical radiographs, panoramic images, intraoral photographs, and diagnostic study models.
  • Treatment Plans & Progress Notes: Proposed and completed restorative procedures (fillings, crowns, bridges), cosmetic care, periodontal therapy, clinical anesthetic details, and materials utilized.
  • Dental Laboratory Work Orders: Custom laboratory prescriptions and shade-matching information provided to accredited dental laboratories for fabrication of crowns, bridges, nightguards, or dental prosthetics.
  • Billing & Insurance Records: Dental subscriber numbers, insurance benefit claims, explanation of benefits (EOBs), payment receipts, and balance ledgers.
Clinical Operations

3. How We Use & Disclose Dental Health Information

The following categories describe the different ways McKinley Road Dental uses and discloses medical and dental health information without requiring your prior written authorization:

For Dental Treatment

We use your dental health records to provide, coordinate, and manage your oral health care. For example, Dr. Sydlowski reviews your medical history, digital X-rays, and periodontal pocket records to formulate treatment plans. We may consult with or share diagnostic records with referring specialists (such as endodontists, periodontists, or oral surgeons) or commercial dental laboratories fabricating your crowns.

For Payment & Insurance

We disclose necessary dental information to bill and collect payment from you, your dental insurance carrier, or a third-party payer. For example, we submit diagnostic radiographic images, tooth-specific treatment codes (CDT codes), and narratives to your insurance company to support reimbursement claims for restorations or periodontal scaling.

For Healthcare Operations

We use your records to evaluate the quality of care provided in our Ottawa office, maintain instrument sterilization and biological spore testing logs, train clinical personnel, and comply with state licensing guidelines administered by the Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR).

Appointment Reminders & Recalls

We may contact you by phone, voicemail, text message, or mail to remind you of an upcoming visit or notify you when it is time for your routine six-month preventive cleaning and examination. You may specify your preferred communication channel at our front desk.

Special Circumstances & Legal Disclosures

Under specific legal mandates, we may disclose your health information without your consent:

  • Public Health & Infection Tracking: As required by law to public health authorities for preventing or controlling disease, injury, or disability, or reporting adverse reactions to dental anesthetics or medical devices.
  • Health Oversight Activities: Disclosures to state licensing boards or health oversight agencies for audits, investigations, inspections, and licensure compliance.
  • Judicial & Administrative Proceedings: In response to a valid court order, subpoena, or administrative summons.
  • Law Enforcement & Emergency Situations: To avert a serious, imminent threat to your health or safety, or the health and safety of others.
  • Worker's Compensation: Information related to work-related dental injuries as authorized by Illinois worker's compensation laws.
Statutory Rights

4. Your Rights Regarding Your Dental Records

Under HIPAA and Illinois law (735 ILCS 5/8-2001), you hold substantial rights regarding your protected dental health information:

  • Right to Inspect and Copy Dental Records: You have the right to inspect and obtain a paper or electronic copy of your complete dental records, including clinical chart notes, periodontal measurements, and digital radiographs (X-rays). Written requests should be directed to our Ottawa office. In accordance with Illinois law, we may charge a reasonable, cost-based administrative fee for duplicating radiographs and physical records.
  • Right to Request an Amendment: If you believe information in your dental chart is inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date, you may request an amendment in writing detailing the reason for your request. If we approve the request, we will amend your record; if we deny it (for example, if the record accurately reflects the clinical findings at the time of examination), we will provide you with a written explanation.
  • Right to Restrict Disclosures (Self-Pay Rule): If you pay in full out-of-pocket for a specific dental procedure or service, you have the absolute statutory right under HIPAA to request that information regarding that procedure not be disclosed to your dental health plan or insurer.
  • Right to Request Confidential Communications: You have the right to request that we communicate with you regarding your appointments and dental care through specific alternative channels (e.g., calling your mobile phone instead of a home phone, or mailing statements to an alternate address). We accommodate all reasonable requests.
  • Right to an Accounting of Disclosures: You may request an accounting list of non-routine disclosures of your dental information made by our practice for purposes other than treatment, payment, or healthcare operations.
  • Right to a Paper Copy of This Notice: You are entitled to receive a physical paper copy of this complete Notice of Privacy Practices at any time upon request at our Ottawa front desk.
  • Right to File a Complaint: If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you have the right to file a formal complaint with our Practice Privacy Officer or with the Secretary of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) Office for Civil Rights. McKinley Road Dental will never retaliate or alter your care in any manner for filing a complaint.

Urgent Care

9. Emergency Dental Care Protocol

Do Not Use Online Forms for Dental Emergencies

Our website appointment forms and email inbox are not continuously monitored 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. If you are experiencing a severe, acute dental or medical emergency, do not wait for an electronic reply.

If you experience any of the following acute symptoms:

  • Severe, spreading facial swelling or difficulty swallowing or breathing
  • Uncontrolled bleeding following an oral injury or tooth extraction
  • Acute, severe dental trauma, knocked-out (avulsed) permanent tooth, or jaw fracture
  • Excruciating, debilitating pain not relieved by standard palliative care

Please take immediate action by:

  1. Calling our Ottawa office directly at (815) 433-3996 during operating hours for emergency scheduling triage.
  2. Visiting the nearest emergency department, such as OSF Saint Elizabeth Medical Center Emergency Department in Ottawa, IL.
  3. Dialing 911 immediately if experiencing airway compromise, severe trauma, or life-threatening symptoms.
Operatory Standards

10. Clinical Operatory & Infection Control Standards

The health, safety, and physical well-being of our patients and clinical team are the foundational pillars of our practice. McKinley Road Dental maintains rigorous infection control and operatory safety protocols that meet or exceed all federal and state guidelines:

Autoclave Sterilization & Spore Testing

All reusable dental instruments and handpieces undergo multi-stage hospital-grade ultrasonic cleaning and high-temperature pressurized steam autoclave sterilization. We perform independent biological spore testing weekly to verify sterilization efficacy and maintain strict sterilization logs.

EPA-Registered Operatory Disinfection

Between each patient visit, treatment operatories are thoroughly sanitized using EPA-registered intermediate-level hospital disinfectants. All clinical contact surfaces, chair controls, dental units, and delivery systems are disinfected and covered with single-use protective barriers.

CDC, OSHA & ADA Adherence

Our clinical practices comply strictly with the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) Guidelines for Infection Control in Dental Health-Care Settings, the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) Bloodborne Pathogens Standard, and American Dental Association standards.

Single-Use Disposable Supplies

Where sterilization is not applicable, including saliva ejector tips, prophy angles, suction cannulas, anesthetic needles, and patient bibs, items are strictly single-use and disposed of in accordance with Illinois medical waste regulations.

Radiology Protocol

11. Digital Radiography & Radiation Protection

Diagnostic dental imaging is essential for detecting tooth decay between teeth, assessing periodontal bone levels, evaluating tooth roots, and checking the bone structure of the jaw.

  • Low-Dose Digital Radiography: We utilize modern digital dental X-ray sensors, which reduce patient radiation exposure by up to 80–90% compared to traditional dental film.
  • ALARA Principle: We strictly follow the ALARA principle (As Low As Reasonably Achievable), ordering diagnostic radiographs only when clinically necessary to evaluate oral health conditions.
  • Protective Shielding: We provide lead aprons and protective thyroid collars for patients during radiographic imaging procedures.
  • Regulatory Compliance: All radiographic equipment is registered, calibrated, and inspected in strict adherence to regulations set forth by the Illinois Emergency Management Agency (IEMA) Division of Nuclear Safety.

12. Scheduling, Cancellation & Insurance Estimates

Appointment Scheduling & Courtesy Notice Policy

Because we value your time and strive to maintain on-time appointments with minimal waiting times, our practice reserves specific operatory time and clinical staff exclusively for your scheduled visit.

48-Hour Rescheduling Courtesy

We kindly request at least 24 to 48 hours advance notice if you need to reschedule or cancel an appointment. This courtesy allows our team to offer that clinical appointment slot to other patients in Ottawa and LaSalle County who may be in urgent need of dental care.

Dental Insurance & Financial Estimates

Our office team is pleased to assist patients in navigating dental insurance benefits, verifying policy provisions, and submitting claims directly to insurance providers on your behalf. Please understand the following practical guidelines:

  • Estimates Are Not Guarantees: Any calculation of insurance coverage or estimated patient copayment provided prior to treatment is a good-faith approximation based on current information provided by your insurance company. Actual benefits are determined solely by your insurer upon receipt of the clinical claim.
  • Patient Financial Responsibility: Dental insurance is a contract between the patient, their employer, and the insurance carrier. Patients are ultimately responsible for payment of all deductibles, copayments, coinsurance, and non-covered services rendered in our office at the time of service, unless prior financial arrangements have been confirmed in writing.
